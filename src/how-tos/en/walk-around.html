Add a character and walk it around your world with the keyboard or on-screen controls. Add a player

Open Snippets and drag the player snippet onto the workspace. It adds a character called player, gives it physics, and makes the camera follow it.

Make it move

Open Snippets, then Movement, and drag the forward and backward snippet onto the workspace. It makes the player walk forward and backward when you press the controls, and stand still when you let go.

If you want your character to walk left and right too, use the second movement snippet instead.

Walk around

Click Play. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or WASD to move. On a touchscreen, use the on-screen D-pad. With a game controller, use the left stick.

Adjust the camera

In the "camera follow" block, change distance to move the camera closer to or further from your character. Change angle to raise or lower it: 90 looks at your character from the side, and smaller numbers look down from higher up.

Tick front to start with the camera facing your character instead of behind it. This is helpful if you designed your character before adding the camera, so you see it the same way you did while building it.

In the second movement snippet, try changing sideways to strafe in the move blocks. Sideways turns your character to face the way it walks; strafe slides it left or right while it keeps facing the same way.
