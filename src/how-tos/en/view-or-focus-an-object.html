View an object

Select a block that made an object and click View in its block menu. The camera orbits around that object on the canvas, so you can look at it from every side. With the keyboard, press V on a focused block.

Click View again, or press V again, to stop orbiting and go back to the camera you were using before.

Focus on an object

Focus selects the object and moves the camera to look at it, then leaves you in control. Right-click the block — on a touchscreen, tap and hold — and choose Focus. With the keyboard, press J on a focused block.

If the camera is following a player, the player moves next to the object and faces it. Otherwise the camera moves so the object is in view.

Which one to use

Use View when you want to look closely at one object from every side. Use Focus when you want to find an object and select it, ready to work on it with a gizmo such as Position, or carry on exploring.

Sometimes you'll want both: Focus to bring the object into view, then View to look at it more closely.
