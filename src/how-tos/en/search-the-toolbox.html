Find the block you need without looking through every category. Search

Click the Search box at the top of the toolbox and type part of a block's name, like star or say. The matching blocks appear next to the toolbox, ready to drag into your code.

With the keyboard, press T to go to the toolbox, then Ctrl+F to go to the Search box, and type. Press Tab to move to the results, use the arrow keys to pick a block and press Enter to add it to your code.

Clear the search

Click the × in the Search box to clear it and go back to the categories.
