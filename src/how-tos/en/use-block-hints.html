Find out what a block does without leaving your code. What block hints are

A block hint is a short description of what a block does. When hints are on, selecting a block shows its hint in a box at the top right of the code area.

Turn hints on

Click the Block hints button below the code area. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+B then 9 to jump to it, and press Enter.

Turn hints off

Click the Block hints button again, or the close button on the hint box. With the keyboard, press Enter on the button again.

Flock XR remembers whether you had hints on or off next time you visit.
