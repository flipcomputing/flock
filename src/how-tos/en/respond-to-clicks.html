Add a when clicked block

Add a when clicked block from Events, choose your object, and put blocks inside.

With the keyboard: go close to the object and press E or 2 when the interact indicator appears.

Clicking again

Clicking the same object again does nothing until the blocks inside have finished. await makes the glide wait until it ends, so the star can't be clicked again while it moves.

Something different each click

Press the plus button at the bottom of the when clicked block to add a then section. The first click runs the blocks at the top, the next click runs the then section, and after the last section the next click starts again from the top. With the keyboard: move to the plus button with the arrow keys and press Enter.

The star glides up on one click and back down on the next.

Each click waits for the section before it to finish. Press the minus button next to a then to remove that section.

Many objects

One when clicked block works for every object an add block makes in a loop, or every object with a tag. Inside it, the name means the object that was clicked.
