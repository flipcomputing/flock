Make your code bigger to read it, or smaller to see more of it at once. Zoom the code area

Click Zoom in to make the blocks bigger, and Zoom out to make them smaller. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+B then 9 to jump to the buttons below the code area, press Tab to reach the zoom buttons and press Enter.

Zoom everything

To make all of Flock XR bigger or smaller — menus, panels and text too — use your web browser's zoom. In most browsers, press Ctrl++ to zoom in, Ctrl+- to zoom out and Ctrl+0 to go back to normal.
