Play your projects with a games controller. Connect a controller

You can use any gamepad-compatible controller, such as a PlayStation controller or a PlayStation adaptive controller. Connect it to your device with a cable or Bluetooth, then press a button on it so your web browser notices it.

Play with it

Once it's connected, just start using the controller to move and look around. The Player Controls tab shows what each stick and button does.
