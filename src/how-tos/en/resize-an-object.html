Make an object bigger or smaller. Choose the resize gizmo

Click Resize in the gizmo bar. If an object is already selected, the resize gizmo attaches to it. Otherwise, click the object you want to resize on the canvas.

Drag a handle

Drag one of the handles and let go when the object is the right size. Each handle stretches it along one axis:

  • The blue X handle changes its width.
  • The green Y handle changes its height.
  • The orange Z handle changes its depth.

Drag the grey box in the middle to resize it the same amount in every direction, so it keeps its shape.

Use canvas controls

Use the on-screen gizmo controls: tap the X, Y, or Z button for the axis you want to resize along, or = to resize it evenly, then use the - and + buttons to change its size.

Show or hide gizmo controls from Menu > Tools > Gizmo controls.

Use the keyboard

With the resize gizmo on an object, press X, Y or Z to pick an axis, or = or U to resize it evenly. Then press the arrow keys to make it bigger or smaller. Hold Shift for smaller steps. Press Enter when you have finished, or Esc to put the gizmo away.

Switch objects

Click on another object to move the resize gizmo to it.

Put the gizmo away

When you have finished, click off the canvas or click the Resize button again.
