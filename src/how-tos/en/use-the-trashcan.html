Delete blocks you don't need, and get them back if you change your mind. The trashcan

The trashcan sits in the corner of the code area.

Delete blocks

Drag blocks onto the trashcan, or back onto the toolbox, to delete them. You can also select a block and click Delete in its block menu. With the keyboard, press Delete or Backspace on a focused block.

A full trashcan

When the trashcan has deleted blocks in it, it fills up.

 Get blocks back

Click the trashcan to open it and see the blocks you've deleted, then drag a block out and drop it where you want it. Click the trashcan again to close it.

With the keyboard, press Tab until the trashcan is focused and press Enter to open it. Use the arrow keys to pick a block and press Enter, then use the arrow keys to move it and press Enter again to put it down. Press Esc to close the trashcan.

The trashcan is emptied when you reload the page, so get back anything you need before then. To bring back something you only just deleted, you can also use Undo.
