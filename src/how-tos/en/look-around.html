Move through your scene and look at it from different angles. Move around

On a keyboard, use WASD or the arrow keys to move, and R and F to fly up and down. On a touchscreen, use the on-screen controls — button 1 flies up and button 3 flies down.

Look around

On a computer, hold the left mouse button down and drag on the scene to rotate the camera. On a touchscreen, drag with one finger.

Reset the view

Click Play to run your project again and reset the view.

When you create a new project in Flock XR it uses the fly camera, which you can use to move around whether you're editing or playing your world.
