Add a group

Drag an add group block from Connect into your workspace. Then drag the blocks for the objects you want to group inside it.

Use gizmos on the group

When active is ticked, the gizmos work on the whole group. Click any object in the group with Position, Rotate or Resize and the gizmo attaches to the group, so all the objects in it move, turn or resize together.

A group resizes the same amount in every direction, so it keeps its shape.

 Change one object in the group

Untick active on the group block. The gizmos then work on each object on its own. Tick active again when you have finished, and the objects move together again.

Line up the objects

Click the align button on the group block to line up the objects in the group or space them out evenly.
