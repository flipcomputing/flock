Run your project to see your code in action, and stop it when you need to. Play

Click Play to run your code and build your scene. Click it again after changing your code to start again with your changes. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+P.

Stop

Click Stop to stop your project. You only need to stop it if you want the sound to stop, or if things moving in the scene are distracting while you work. Your project needs to be running to use the gizmos, so press Play again before using them.
