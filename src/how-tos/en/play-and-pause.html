Play

Click Play to run your code and build your scene. Click it again after changing your code to start again with your changes. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+P.

Pause

Click Pause to pause your project. Everything stops moving and the sound goes quiet, but you can still look around and use the gizmos. Click Pause again to unpause your code and continue from where you left off, or click Play to start again from the beginning. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+K to pause and again to unpause your code. It works while you're working on your code too.
