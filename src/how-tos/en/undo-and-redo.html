Take back a change you didn't mean to make, or put it back again. Undo

Click Undo below the code area to take back your last change. You can undo many times in a row, one change at a time, to go further and further back. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+Z.

Redo

Click Redo to put back a change you just undid. You can redo many times too, until you're back where you started. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+Shift+Z.

On a phone

On a phone held upright, Undo and Redo also appear under the canvas, next to the gizmo buttons, so you can undo while you're working on your scene.
