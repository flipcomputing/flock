Keep your project safe in a file, and open it again later. Save a project

Click Projects, then Save, to save your project as a file on your computer. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+S.

Flock XR keeps your work in this web browser as you go, but that isn't safe for long. Save to a file to keep your project, or to move it to another computer. Open a project

Click Projects, then Open, and pick a Flock XR project file. You can also drag the file straight onto Flock XR. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+O.
