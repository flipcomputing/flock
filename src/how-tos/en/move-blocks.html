Move a whole stack of blocks, or just one block out of a stack. Move a stack

Drag a block to move it together with all the blocks below it. With the keyboard, press Shift+M on a focused block, use the arrow keys to move the stack and press Enter to put it down.

Move a single block

Hold Ctrl while you drag a block. Only that block moves — the blocks below it close up the gap and stay where they are. With the keyboard, press M on a focused block, use the arrow keys to move it and press Enter to put it down.

Cut and paste

Press Ctrl+X to cut a block — only that block is cut, and the blocks below it close up the gap. Select the block you want it to follow, then press Ctrl+V to paste it in its new position.
