Use the block menu to do the things you do most with a block. Open the block menu

Click or tap a block to open its block menu. With the keyboard, press H on a focused block or one of its fields. The same click or key closes it again. Only the buttons that make sense for that block are shown.

Collapse

Collapse folds a whole script, like a start block, into one line to save space. With the keyboard, press C.

Expand

Expand opens a collapsed block back up. With the keyboard, press C.

Unlock

Unlock appears on a locked block, so you can change it again. With the keyboard, press L.

Duplicate

Duplicate makes a copy of the block next to it, ready to drag somewhere else. With the keyboard, press D.

Copy

Copy copies the block so you can paste it somewhere else. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+C.

Paste

Paste appears once you've copied something, and adds the copy straight after the selected block. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+V.

Detach

Detach takes the block out of its stack so it sits on its own. With the keyboard, press X.

Disable and enable

Disable switches a block off, so it's skipped when your code runs. Click it again to switch the block back on. With the keyboard, press E.

View

View appears on a block that made an object, and orbits the camera around that object on the canvas. Click it again to stop. With the keyboard, press V.

Delete

Delete removes the block. With the keyboard, press Delete.

Edit a block

Clicking a block's fields, or the values plugged into it, leaves the menu closed, so it stays out of the way while you edit.

See every action

For the full list of actions, right-click a block — on a touchscreen, tap and hold. With the keyboard, press Ctrl+Enter on a focused block.
