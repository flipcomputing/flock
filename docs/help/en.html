The block menu

Click or tap a block to open its block menu — buttons for the things you do most, such as duplicating, commenting on or deleting that block. When a block is focused, press H to open the menu from the keyboard — H on one of its fields opens the same menu. The same click or key closes it again.

Clicking a block's fields, or the values plugged into it, leaves the menu closed, so it stays out of the way while you edit. For the full list of actions, right-click a block — on a touchscreen, tap and hold.
