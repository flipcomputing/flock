# Case studies and shout outs

Educators and community members have written about and shared their feedback and experience of using Flock XR in clubs, classrooms and workshops. A selection is collected here.

## Case studies

- **[Programming 3D Worlds in Flock XR with Students with Social, Emotional and Mental Health Needs](https://sheffieldclc.net/programming-3d-worlds-in-flock-xr-with-students-with-social-emotional-and-mental-health-needs/)**
  — Catherine Elliott, SEN specialist teacher, Sheffield eLearning Service. Describes an
  introductory Flock XR lesson with year 8-9 students at a special school, and how the tool's
  immediate visual feedback and customisation options were motivating for students with SEMH
  needs.
  > "Flock XR is a great application to work with learners with SEMH needs in upper primary and
  > secondary schools, being engaging and easily customisable. It is simple enough to allow for
  > students to create something meaningful quickly, with plenty of scope for teaching more
  > complex concepts such as selection and variables as learners progress."

  > "They all enjoyed it! The students were engaged and motivated by the 3D element of the
  > software and enjoyed testing its boundaries to see what they could do." — class teacher

- **[My Experience of Flock XR and Teaching Game Development](https://www.interactiveclassroom.net/?p=2110)**
  — Nicki Cooper, Computing Curriculum Specialist, Interactive Classroom. A computing teacher's
  experience using Flock XR as a successor to the discontinued Kodu Game Lab, recreating classic
  arcade games like Pong and Pacman to teach fundamental programming concepts.
  > "Flock is a free and open-source 3D creation software that runs in the web browser."
  >
  > "Games can always be extended, improved or tweaked so are perfect for all abilities."

- **[Working holiday in Harrow at the Digital Schoolhouse 2025 conference](https://cambolc.blog/2025/07/02/humpday-post-working-holiday-in-harrow-at-the-digital-schoolhouse-2025-conference-500-pennies-of-thought/)**
  — Philip Spalding, Teacher. Recounts introducing Year 7 and 8 pupils at an independent school in
  Cambridge to Flock XR as a next step from Scratch, just days after discovering it himself.
  > "So with the Year 7 and Year 8 pupils in the Independent School in Cambridge we embarked on the
  > next step from Scratch. Within a few minutes after brief instructions we had characters leaping
  > and bounding about the screen and a room of engaged and happy pupils learning individually and
  > collectively from each other."

- **[My experience of the Playful Computing Conference](https://www.interactiveclassroom.net/?p=2004&utm_source=rss&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=playful-computing-conference)**
  — Nicki Cooper, Computing Curriculum Specialist, Interactive Classroom.
  > "The next session I attended was a hands-on session, in which we were able to try out new
  > online software by Flip Computing, called Flock XR. This is a lovely visual coding
  > environment, in which you can create your own 3D animations and games."

- **[CKC 2025 write-up](https://www.computingatschool.org.uk/forum-news-blogs/2025/july/ckc2025-1/)**
  — Allen Tsui, Primary School Teacher, Computing at School.'
  live-coding demo of Flock XR at CKC 2025.
  > "My favourite 'wow!' moment though was from Tracy Gardner and Rebecca Franks live coding
  > experience of Flip Computing's FlockXR. For those who have yet to try, Tracy and Rebecca
  > described the web based platform as a perfect opportunity to bridge 'the gap between
  > block-based tools and professional platforms like Unity and UEFN, all within a user-friendly
  > browser-based interface' and 'transition from Scratch style programming to working in...'
  > well, you'll just have to have a go yourselves."

  Allen was also a guest speaker at the July 2025 Flock XR Community Meetup — see the video below.
