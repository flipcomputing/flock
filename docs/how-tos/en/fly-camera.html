Take control of the camera and fly anywhere in your world. Switch to the fly camera

Click Camera in the gizmo menu to switch from the play camera to the fly camera.

Fly around

On a keyboard, use WASD or the arrow keys to move, and R and F to fly up and down (Page Up and Page Down work too). On a touchscreen, use the on-screen controls — button 1 flies up and button 3 flies down. With a game controller, use the left stick to move and the right stick to look around.

Look around

On a computer, hold the left mouse button down and drag on the scene to look around. On a touchscreen, drag with one finger.

Hand control back

Click Camera again to return to the play camera and hand control back to your project.

If you were orbiting an object, the first click steps back out to your previous view instead — click Camera once more to fly.
