Build a scene with a gradient sky and a ground map. Open a new project

Click Projects, then New.

Add a scene snippet

Click Snippets in the toolbox. Click or drag the sky and map snippet into your workspace.

Change the sky colors

Click a color on the sky block in your workspace to change it.

Change the map

Try choosing different options for the map block in your workspace.

You can change the shape of the ground, its pattern, and its color.

 Play your scene

Click Play to run your project and see the scene appear.

 Drag the resizer bar between the canvas and the code panel to give either one more room to work in.
