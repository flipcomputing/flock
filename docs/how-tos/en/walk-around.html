Step into your world and move around in it. Enter Play mode

Click Play to switch from editing your project to exploring it.

 Move and look around

On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or WASD to move and the mouse to look around. On a touchscreen, use the on-screen D-pad and buttons. With a game controller, use the left stick to move and the right stick to look around.

Interact with objects

Press the interact button (E, or Button 2 on a controller) near an object to interact with it, if your project responds to it.

The Player tab in this panel lists every control for keyboard, touch, and game controller, including the screen-reader shortcuts.
