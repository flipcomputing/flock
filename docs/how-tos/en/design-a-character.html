Bring a character into your world and make it your own. Load a character

In the Scene category, drag a "load character" block into your start stack. Choose a character model from its dropdown.

Name your character

Give your character a name in its ID field. You'll use this name later to make it move, talk, or react to things.

Style and animate it

Open the Materials category to change the character's color or texture. Try the Animate category to give it an animation, like idle or walk.

Click or tap a block to open its block menu, or press H when a block is focused. Click the info button for block info to see what the focused block does.
