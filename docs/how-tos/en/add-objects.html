Add objects to your scene. Add an object

To add an object to your scene:

  • Click Add to open the object menu.
  • Pick one of the objects. Use the arrow buttons on the sides of each row to see more.
  • Click a surface on the canvas to place your object there. The code block for your object appears with your blocks, so your scene and your code stay in step.
Add more objects

Repeat the steps above to add more objects to your scene. To reach other sides of your world, open the Explore your world how-to and move around to add objects in different places.

You can also add objects from the toolbox: drag a shape block out of the Scene category and drop it into a start script — there can be more than one. Press Play to run your project again and reset the view.
