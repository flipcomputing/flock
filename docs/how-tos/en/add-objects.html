Fill your scene with shapes and ready-made models to explore. Add a shape

In the Scene category, drag out a shape block — a box, sphere, star, or heart — and drop it into your start stack.

Position it

Set its position using the X, Y, and Z fields on the block, or turn on gizmo hints from the Tools menu and drag it in the 3D view.

Add a ready-made model

For more variety, use the "load model" block to bring in a ready-made object instead of a plain shape.

Duplicate objects

Right-click a block (press and hold on a touchscreen) to duplicate it. Now you can quickly place copies of the same object around your scene.

X moves an object left and right, Y moves it up and down, and Z moves it forward and backward.
